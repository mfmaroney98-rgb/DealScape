Company Overview

Founded in 2004, Heartland Commercial HVAC & Facilities Services is a premier provider of commercial mechanical maintenance, preventative HVAC servicing, and building automation support across Central Ohio.

Approximately 68% of revenue is derived from recurring multi-year preventative maintenance contracts with regional hospital networks, school districts, and Class-A office parks. The business is 100% founder-owned and operated, and the owner is seeking a full exit due to retirement.
